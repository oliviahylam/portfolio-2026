## The brief

Amazon Ads had refreshed its Programmatic Solutions Certifications and needed short LinkedIn feed assets to announce them. The goal was to make partners, advertisers and agencies feel that certifying keeps them ahead of the curve, and to drive them to Amazon Ads Academy to certify or recertify.

## Working inside the brand

Every asset had to follow Amazon Ads brand guidelines, and colour is a core part of them. I kept each post on Squid Ink with Smile Orange as the single accent, so the orange always marks the one thing that matters: the headline word, or the block that stands apart.

## The workflow

I generated all of the visual assets with AI: the 3D blocks, the isometric illustrations and the photography. Then I built each post myself, setting the headlines, hierarchy and layout with typography so the generated imagery and Amazon Ads' type read as one system. Working this way let me explore three distinct creative directions within a single bespoke brief.

## Direction 1 — 3D blocks

Soft white blocks with a single orange one: a simple metaphor for standing apart from the crowd and staying ahead. This was the direction the client enjoyed most.
